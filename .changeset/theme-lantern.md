theme-lantern: add the theme

- An enterprise product on a light grey page with a daybreak blue, six-pixel corners and a system
  face.
- The neutral ramp is built from the composited surfaces, fills, inks and lines of each mode.
- Every ramp is written in OKLCH and keyed by its own steps, and every role sits on a step of its
  ramp. The theme's specification runs it through `violations` at 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for lines
  and rings, in both modes.
