theme-compass: add the theme and its high-contrast variant

- A team product on soft neutrals with a bright blue, a lime success and small corners.
- `compass-contrast` places every surface, ink, line and role again, further from the page.
- Every ramp is written in OKLCH and keyed by its own steps, and every role sits on a step of its
  ramp. The theme's specification runs it through `violations` at 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for lines
  and rings, in both modes.
