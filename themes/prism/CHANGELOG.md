# @stealthscale/theme-prism

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- [#32](https://github.com/stealth-scale/config/pull/32) [`74f058f`](https://github.com/stealth-scale/config/commit/74f058f72bc61f672a46f671fc0d36e9e3f68d35) Thanks [@stealth-rklopper](https://github.com/stealth-rklopper)! - theme-prism: add the theme
  
  - A creative tool on cool greys with an indigo-blue accent, eight-pixel corners and Source Sans.
  - Every hue has sixteen steps and the neutral ramp thirteen, each with a light ramp and a dark one.
  - Every ramp is written in OKLCH and keyed by its own steps, and every role sits on a step of its
    ramp. The theme's specification runs it through `violations` at 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for lines
    and rings, in both modes.
