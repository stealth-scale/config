  - An enterprise product on plain greys with a cobalt blue, square corners and IBM Plex.
  - `steel-gray` moves the page one step in from each end of the neutral ramp.
  - Every ramp is written in OKLCH and keyed by its own steps, and every role sits on a step of its

    and rings, in both modes.
